# Демонстрации сравнения — ImageGen

Встроенный ImageGen. Каждая пара создана как два кадра одного ракурса. Исходники: assets/originals/comparison/. Оптимизированные WebP: public/images/comparison/. Фотографии не являются работами студии.

## polish

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset: two precisely aligned automotive demonstration photographs as a VERTICAL CONTACT SHEET, exactly 2 equal full-width frames stacked vertically, no gaps, no borders, no captions, no text or logos. Overall aspect 3:4 portrait (ideally 1536x2048); each frame landscape 3:2. The UPPER frame shows BEFORE and the LOWER frame shows AFTER. BOTH frames depict exactly the SAME subject, camera, framing, light placement, background, perspective, and lens. It is a controlled automotive visualization, premium photographic quality, graphite-black studio, cool neutral realistic light. No people or tools intruding into the scene. No artificial graphic lines, no diagonal overlays, no split screen within individual frames. The subject must remain perfectly registered between the upper and lower frame. Subject: close-up of the broad curved piano-black hood of a black BMW M5 F90, looking diagonally toward one corner of the hood and a small portion of its front headlight. Beautiful real white strip light reflections across the lacquer and soft studio background. UPPER: authentic fine circular swirl scratches and light wash marring in the clear coat, visible under a controlled inspection light, slightly hazy reflection. The scratch marks must look random, fine and microscopic, never like straight ruled lines. LOWER: identical hood after correction, deep glossy jet-black clear coat, no swirl marks, crisp clean white strip reflections. The image is about the SURFACE, no polisher machine, no hands, no fake extreme damage.
